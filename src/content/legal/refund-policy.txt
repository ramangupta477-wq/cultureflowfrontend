1. Introduction
This Refund & Cancellation Policy ("Policy") applies to all programmes offered by Culture Flow Private Limited (hereinafter referred to as "Culture Flow," "we," "us," or "our"), a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 bearing CIN U85500DL2026PTC471392, with its registered office at H No. B-71/B, G/F, Block-B, New Ashok Nagar, East Delhi - 110096, Delhi, India.
Culture Flow designs, facilitates, and manages structured short-term academic immersion programmes at international partner institutions. Our programme fees are all-inclusive, covering academic programme costs, international flights, visa processing, accommodation, meals, local transport, comprehensive insurance, and 24/7 on-ground support.
Given the nature of international academic programmes - which involve significant advance commitments to airlines, partner institutions, accommodation providers, insurance companies, and destination management partners - this Policy establishes fair and transparent terms governing cancellations, refunds, transfers, and related matters.
By enrolling in a Culture Flow programme or making any payment towards a programme, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the terms of this Policy.
2. Definitions
"Programme": Any structured short-term academic immersion offered by Culture Flow at an international partner institution, operating under the 40/40/20 curriculum model.
"Participant" or "Scholar": Any individual enrolled or seeking enrolment in a Culture Flow programme.
"Parent/Guardian": The parent or legal guardian of a Participant who is a minor (under the age of 18), and/or the individual responsible for fee payment.
"Institutional Partner": A school, college, or university that has entered into a partnership agreement with Culture Flow to send a cohort of scholars on a programme.
"Programme Fee": The total all-inclusive fee payable for participation in a Culture Flow programme, as communicated at the time of enrolment.
"Programme Start Date": The first day of the programme as specified in the official programme itinerary - typically the date of departure from India.
"Non-Recoverable Costs": Third-party costs already incurred by Culture Flow on behalf of a Participant that cannot be recovered upon cancellation, including but not limited to airline tickets, visa processing fees, accommodation deposits, insurance premiums, and institutional registration fees.
"Written Notice": A cancellation request submitted via email to info@cultureflow.in from the registered email address of the Participant, Parent/Guardian, or authorised institutional representative.
3. Programme Fees & Payment Terms
Culture Flow programme fees are all-inclusive and cover the following components: academic programme and institutional fees, international return flights, visa processing and documentation, accommodation for the full programme duration, meals, local transport at the destination, comprehensive medical and travel insurance, on-ground support and chaperone infrastructure, pre-departure orientation, and post-programme certification.
The exact programme fee, payment schedule, and applicable instalment structure (if any) will be communicated to Participants and their Parent/Guardians at the time of enrolment confirmation. All fees are denominated and payable in Indian Rupees (INR) unless explicitly stated otherwise.
Registration Deposit: A non-refundable registration deposit may be required at the time of enrolment to secure a place in the programme. The amount of this deposit will be specified in the enrolment confirmation and will be adjusted against the total programme fee.
Payment Deadlines: All remaining programme fees must be paid in full by the deadline specified in the payment schedule. Failure to complete payment by the specified deadline may result in forfeiture of the Participant's place in the programme, and any amounts already paid will be subject to the cancellation terms outlined in this Policy.
4. Cancellation by Participant, Parent, or Guardian
Cancellations must be communicated in writing via email to info@cultureflow.in. The date on which Culture Flow receives the Written Notice shall be considered the effective date of cancellation for determining applicable refund amounts.
The following refund schedule applies to cancellations initiated by the Participant, their Parent/Guardian, or any individual acting on their behalf:

| Cancellation Period | Refund Amount | Deduction |
| 60 or more days before programme start | 80% of total programme fee | 20% administrative charge + any non-recoverable third-party costs already incurred |
| 30 to 59 days before programme start | 50% of total programme fee | 50% administrative charge + any non-recoverable third-party costs already incurred |
| 15 to 29 days before programme start | 25% of total programme fee | 75% administrative charge + any non-recoverable third-party costs already incurred |
| Less than 15 days before programme start | No refund | 100% — full programme fee forfeited |
All refund calculations are based on the total programme fee paid as of the date of cancellation. The administrative charge covers programme design, coordination, institutional liaison, and operational costs incurred by Culture Flow up to the point of cancellation.
Where Non-Recoverable Costs already incurred by Culture Flow exceed the applicable deduction percentage, the actual Non-Recoverable Costs will be deducted and only the remaining balance, if any, will be refunded.
5. Cancellation by Institutional Partner (B2B)
Where a programme has been arranged through a partnership agreement with a school, college, or university, the cancellation terms specified in the institutional service agreement shall take precedence over this general Policy to the extent of any inconsistency.
In the absence of specific terms in the institutional agreement, the following shall apply:
Full Cohort Cancellation: If an Institutional Partner cancels the entire cohort, the refund schedule in Section 4 shall apply to the aggregate programme fees, unless the institutional agreement specifies otherwise. Culture Flow will work with the institution to explore deferral to an alternative programme date before processing cancellation.
Partial Withdrawal: If an Institutional Partner withdraws individual scholars from a confirmed cohort, refunds for withdrawn scholars will follow the schedule in Section 4. If partial withdrawal reduces the cohort below the minimum enrolment threshold, Culture Flow reserves the right to cancel or restructure the programme under Section 6.
6. Cancellation or Modification by Culture Flow
Culture Flow reserves the right to cancel, postpone, or modify a programme under the following circumstances:
6.1 Minimum Enrolment Not Met
Each programme requires a minimum number of enrolled Participants to be operationally and financially viable. If the minimum enrolment threshold is not met by the specified deadline, Culture Flow may cancel the programme. In such cases, Participants will receive a full refund (100%) of all programme fees paid, processed within 21 business days of the cancellation announcement. Alternatively, Participants may choose to defer to the next available programme at no additional cost, subject to availability.
6.2 Safety, Regulatory, or Operational Reasons
Culture Flow may cancel or modify a programme if conditions at the destination - including but not limited to political instability, health emergencies, natural disasters, travel advisories issued by the Government of India or the destination country, or regulatory changes - make it unsafe, impractical, or legally impermissible to operate the programme as planned.
In such cases, Culture Flow will offer Participants the choice of: (a) a full refund of all programme fees paid, less any Non-Recoverable Costs that cannot be recovered from third-party providers; or (b) deferral to an alternative programme or future cohort at no additional programme fee, subject to availability and any differential costs.
6.3 Force Majeure
Neither Culture Flow nor the Participant shall be liable for failure to perform obligations under this Policy if such failure results from events beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to: acts of God, pandemics, epidemics, war, terrorism, civil unrest, government-imposed travel restrictions, airline strikes, natural disasters, or any other event constituting force majeure under Indian law.
In force majeure situations, Culture Flow will make every reasonable effort to recover costs from third-party providers and pass any recovered amounts on to the Participant. The refund in such circumstances will be the total programme fee paid, less actual Non-Recoverable Costs that Culture Flow is unable to recover despite best efforts.
6.4 Participant Conduct
Culture Flow reserves the right to withdraw a Participant from a programme - before or during the programme - if the Participant violates the mandatory Code of Conduct, engages in behaviour that jeopardises the safety or wellbeing of other scholars or staff, or fails to comply with programme rules and local laws. In such cases, no refund shall be payable.
7. Non-Refundable Components
The following costs, once incurred, are non-refundable regardless of the reason for cancellation:
Registration Deposit: The initial non-refundable deposit paid at the time of enrolment, as specified in the enrolment confirmation.
Visa Processing Fees: Embassy and consulate fees, VFS/visa application centre charges, and any associated documentation costs once the visa application has been submitted.
Airline Tickets: Non-refundable airfare or the cancellation penalty charged by the airline, whichever is applicable, once tickets have been issued.
Insurance Premiums: Medical and travel insurance premiums once the policy has been activated or the coverage period has commenced.
Institutional Registration Fees: Any registration or enrolment fees paid to international partner institutions that are non-refundable under the institution's own policies.
Accommodation Deposits: Non-refundable deposits or cancellation charges levied by accommodation providers.
Culture Flow will provide a transparent breakdown of Non-Recoverable Costs upon request at the time of processing any cancellation.
8. Refund Processing
Processing Timeline: All eligible refunds will be processed within 21 business days from the date Culture Flow receives the Written Notice of cancellation and all required documentation.
Mode of Refund: Refunds will be credited to the same bank account or payment method from which the original payment was received, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
Currency: All refunds will be processed in Indian Rupees (INR), regardless of any currency conversion that may have occurred during the original payment.
Refund Confirmation: Culture Flow will issue a written refund confirmation via email, including a breakdown of the refund amount, deductions applied, and the expected timeline for credit.
Any delays in refund processing due to banking procedures, payment gateway timelines, or documentation requirements beyond Culture Flow's control shall not constitute a breach of this Policy.
9. Transfer & Deferral Policy
9.1 Deferral to a Future Cohort
A Participant may request deferral of their enrolment to a future programme cohort, subject to the following conditions:
a. The deferral request must be submitted in writing at least 30 days before the Programme Start Date.
b. Deferral is permitted once per enrolment. A second deferral request will be treated as a cancellation under Section 4.
c. The deferred enrolment must be utilised within 12 months of the original Programme Start Date. Failure to enrol within this period will result in forfeiture of all fees paid.
d. If the programme fee for the deferred cohort is higher than the original fee, the Participant must pay the differential. If the deferred cohort fee is lower, no refund of the difference will be issued.
e. Any Non-Recoverable Costs already incurred for the original programme (such as issued airline tickets or activated insurance) will not be carried over and may be deducted from the transferred amount.
9.2 Transfer to Another Participant
Enrolment in a Culture Flow programme is non-transferable to another individual, except in cases where the Institutional Partner requests a substitution within the same cohort and the substitute meets all eligibility and documentation requirements. Such requests must be made at least 30 days before the Programme Start Date and are subject to Culture Flow's approval. An administrative processing fee may apply.
10. No-Show Policy
A "no-show" occurs when a confirmed Participant fails to appear at the designated departure point on the Programme Start Date without providing prior Written Notice of cancellation.
In the event of a no-show, no refund shall be payable. The full programme fee will be forfeited. Culture Flow bears no responsibility for arranging alternative travel or programme access for no-show Participants.
11. Medical & Emergency Cancellations
Culture Flow recognises that unforeseen medical emergencies or exceptional personal circumstances may necessitate cancellation. In such cases:
a. The Participant or their Parent/Guardian must notify Culture Flow in writing as soon as reasonably possible, accompanied by relevant supporting documentation (medical certificates from a registered medical practitioner, hospital records, death certificates, or other appropriate evidence).
b. Culture Flow will review each case individually and may, at its sole discretion, offer a more favourable refund than the standard schedule in Section 4 - up to a maximum refund of the programme fee less actual Non-Recoverable Costs.
c. Where applicable, Culture Flow will assist the Participant in filing claims with the travel and medical insurance provider included in the programme. Any insurance payout received by the Participant for the cancelled trip shall be considered separately and will not reduce the refund amount owed by Culture Flow.
Culture Flow's decision on medical and emergency cancellation refunds shall be final, made in good faith, and communicated in writing.
12. Partial Withdrawal During Programme
If a Participant voluntarily withdraws from a programme after it has commenced, or is withdrawn by Culture Flow due to a Code of Conduct violation (as described in Section 6.4), no refund shall be payable for the unused portion of the programme.
If a Participant is required to leave a programme due to a medical emergency during the programme, Culture Flow will: (a) arrange safe and supported departure in coordination with local emergency services, chaperones, and the Participant's family; and (b) assist in filing an insurance claim for the interrupted programme. No cash refund from Culture Flow shall apply for the unused programme days, but the comprehensive insurance included in every programme may cover such interruptions.
13. Goods and Services Tax (GST)
All programme fees are inclusive of applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST) as per the prevailing rates under Indian tax law. In the event of a refund, the GST component of the refunded amount will be reversed in accordance with applicable GST regulations. Culture Flow will issue appropriate credit notes as required under the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.
14. Dispute Resolution
In the event of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Policy, the parties shall first attempt to resolve the matter amicably through direct discussion.
Grievance Officer: Any grievances related to refunds or cancellations may be directed to the Grievance Officer at info@cultureflow.in. The Grievance Officer shall acknowledge the complaint within 48 hours and endeavour to resolve it within 30 days of receipt.
Governing Law: This Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.
Jurisdiction: Any disputes that cannot be resolved amicably shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of New Delhi, India.
15. Amendments to This Policy
Culture Flow reserves the right to amend this Policy at any time. Any changes will be published on our website at www.cultureflow.in and communicated to enrolled Participants via email. The revised Policy will take effect from the date of publication.
Amendments will not apply retroactively to enrolments confirmed before the date of the amendment, unless the Participant is offered and accepts more favourable terms under the revised Policy.
